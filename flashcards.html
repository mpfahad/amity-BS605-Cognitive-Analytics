LMR — important topics

From the last revision class focus and the densest exam-ready material in the SLM:

  1. Attitude ABC + characteristics (valence, multiplicity, centrality, acquired/invisible) — Module 2 core.
  2. Goleman EI five competencies (self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills) + workplace relevance.
  3. Self-esteem vs self-efficacy vs self-respect (+ self-concept) — definitions and one example each.
  4. Johari four panes + how to enlarge the open area (feedback + disclosure).
  5. T-E-A model — mutual influence of thoughts, emotions, and actions.
  6. Six Thinking Hats — colour to function (blue/white/red/black/yellow/green).
  7. Attribution trio — fundamental attribution error, actor–observer difference, self-serving bias.
  8. Stereotype vs prejudice + contact as a reduction path.
  9. Assertive skills list (respect, openness, honesty, accountability, self-control, delegation).
  10. Tuckman stages (forming → adjourning) + what happens in storming/norming.
  11. Situational leadership — Blanchard S1–S4 matched to readiness; no one best style.
  12. ICEDIP six phases expanded once (inspiration, clarification, distillation, perspiration, evaluation, incubation).
  13. Cognitive errors — name 4–5 with a one-line example (esp. emotional reasoning, all-or-nothing).
  14. Sociometry — definition, sociogram, star/isolate/net patterns.
  15. Work–life integration vs balance + time-management priorities (important vs urgent).
  16. Ellis ABC for negative feelings; resilience = adversity + positive adaptation; Rokeach terminal vs instrumental values.
