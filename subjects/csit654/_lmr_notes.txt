CSIT654 — LMR priorities (live-class teacher weightage + study materials)

1. 1.1.1 Introduction to Security Attacks — classify the stem as passive or active first.
2. 1.1.2 Services and Mechanism — “what vs how” — service vs mechanism is the classic stem.
3. 1.1.3 Classical Encryption Techniques — define plaintext, ciphertext, key, and the two classical families.
4. 2.1.1 Modern Block Ciphers: Block Ciphers Princi — state block size, key, rounds, and why product ciphers.
5. 2.1.2 Shannon’s Theory of Confusion and Diffusio — define confusion vs diffusion in one line each.
6. 2.1.3 Fiestal Structure — write the Li/Ri recurrence and say decryption = reverse keys.
7. 3.1.1 Introduction of Message Authentication Cod — authentication ≠ encryption — say both explicitly in answers.
8. 3.1.2 Authentication Functions — list the three authentication-function classes.
9. 3.1.3 Authentication Requirements — integrity + authenticity are the core MAC requirements.
10. 4.1.1 Authentication Applications: Kerberos — AS → TGT → TGS → service ticket flow in order.
11. 4.1.2 Overview of Access Control List (ACL) — define ACL as per-object subject→rights list.
12. 4.1.3 Electronic Mail Security: Pretty Good Priv — hybrid encryption + web of trust are the PGP headlines.
13. 5.1.1 Introductory Idea of Intrusion — define intrusion + name the three intruder classes.
14. 5.1.2 Intrusion Detection Techniques — contrast signature vs anomaly; HIDS vs NIDS.
15. 5.1.3 Viruses and Worms — virus vs worm one-line contrast is the highest-yield stem.
